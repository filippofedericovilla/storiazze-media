Storiazze

Storiazze is an Italian channel of short illustrated history stories: one historical figure at a time, told in ink-on-white drawings with a gold accent, as Instagram carousels, short vertical videos and longer YouTube videos.

Storiazze Publisher

Storiazze Publisher is the small tool the Storiazze creator uses to publish their own videos to their own TikTok account. The creator signs in with TikTok, reviews the video, chooses the title, the privacy level and the interaction settings, and confirms before anything is posted. The tool is used only for the Storiazze account.
