Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. Who we are

Storiazze Publisher is a personal publishing tool used by the creator of the Storiazze channel to publish their own videos to their own TikTok account. Contact: Instagram @storiazze.

2. Data we access

  • TikTok profile basics (display name and username), through the user.info.basic permission, only to show which account will receive a post.
  • Authorization tokens issued by TikTok (access token and refresh token), needed to publish on the account.
  • Publishing information: the video, title and settings the creator chooses, and the publish status returned by TikTok.

3. How data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish the creator's own videos to the creator's own account. Tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer and are never sent anywhere except to TikTok. We do not sell, share or disclose any data to third parties, and we do not use analytics, advertising cookies or tracking on this website.

4. Visitors to this website

This website is a static page hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and collects no personal data of its own; GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses according to its own privacy statement.

5. Retention, access and deletion

Tokens are kept until the creator revokes access or deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services), and the stored tokens can be deleted by removing the local token file. For any request about data, contact us on Instagram @storiazze.

6. Children

The tool is not intended for use by anyone under 18 and is operated only by the adult creator of the channel.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
